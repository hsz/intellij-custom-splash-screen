# Custom Splash Screen

Replace your JetBrains IDE's startup splash screen with an image you choose. Pick a bundled image or use your own photo, artwork, or logo.

Works across the **JetBrains IDE family**, including **IntelliJ IDEA, PyCharm, Rider, WebStorm, PhpStorm, GoLand, CLion, and more**.

## Get started

1. Open **Settings | Appearance & Behavior | Custom Splash Screen**.
2. Enable **Use a custom splash image** and choose a bundled image or a local file.
3. Click **Apply** and restart your IDE.

Supports PNG, JPEG, GIF, and BMP files up to 20 MiB and 16 megapixels. To restore the default splash, uncheck the option and click **Apply**.

**Compatibility:** No upper IDE-version restriction. Uses the IDE's internal splash cache. After an IDE update, cache reset, or display-scale change, the default splash may appear once before your image is reapplied.
